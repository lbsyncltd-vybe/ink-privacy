Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it, and there is currently no way to move progress between devices.

What are coins and shards for?

Coins and shards are earned by playing levels. Coins buy packs in the shop, and shards are crafted into tools such as Undo, Erase, Peek and Hint. Nothing in the game is bought with real money.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login screen. The game creates an anonymous ID on first launch purely so leaderboard entries have something to attach to.

Why is the leaderboard empty?

Leaderboards need an internet connection. Levels and daily puzzles all work offline.

How do I change my display name?

Settings, then tap your name. It is the name other players see on the leaderboards.

How do I turn off notifications?

Settings, then the Notifications row. You can also turn them off from your device's own settings.
