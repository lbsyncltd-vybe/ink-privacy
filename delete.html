Ink Merge — Delete your data

Published by LBSync Ltd. Last updated 12 October 2026.

This page explains exactly how to delete the data Ink Merge keeps about your game. The app is published by LBSync Ltd and is available on Google Play and the App Store.

How to request deletion

Email info@lbsync.com with:

  1. Your in-game display name (shown next to your coin balance, default is "Painter <number>")
  2. The platform you play on (iOS App Store or Google Play)
  3. A short note saying "please delete my data"

We respond within 30 days. Most requests are completed within 7 days.

What gets deleted

When you request deletion, we remove the following from our servers:

  • Your anonymous Firebase ID and all data stored under it
  • Your display name on tournament and race leaderboards
  • Your cloud-backed game progress (level, coins, shards, tools, gallery pieces, mission state)
  • Your tournament and speed race scores (past and current week)

What is kept (and why)

  • Aggregate / anonymised crash reports. Firebase crash telemetry is already anonymised and cannot be traced back to you after deletion. We keep it for product diagnostics.
  • Nothing else. We do not keep a copy of your personal data after deletion.

Retention of deleted data

Deleted records are purged from our Firestore database within 7 days of your request. Backups retain the record for an additional 30 days after which it is permanently removed; backups are not restored into the live database.

If you deleted the app

Uninstalling the app does NOT automatically delete your cloud data. Your Firebase ID stays on our servers so your progress can be restored if you reinstall. If you want your data removed, email us using the steps above.
